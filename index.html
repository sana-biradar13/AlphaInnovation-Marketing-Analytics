Dataset Citation & Metric Methodology

Dataset Name: Kaggle Clicks Conversion Tracking (XYZ Company Social Media Ad Campaigns)

Source & Attribution: Anonymized real-world advertising campaign dataset widely cited in marketing analytics and predictive data science (1,143 records, 11 features).

Available Attributes:
ad_id: Unique identifier for each individual ad unit.
xyz_campaign_id: ID associated with each ad campaign of XYZ company (916, 936, 1178).
fb_campaign_id: Facebook advertising platform campaign tracking identifier.
age: Demographic target age group ('30-34', '35-39', '40-44', '45-49').
gender: Target audience gender ('M', 'F').
interest: Audience interest category code (40 unique segments).
Impressions: Number of times each ad was rendered/displayed.
Clicks: Number of times users clicked on the ad unit.
Spent: Exact ad spend ($ USD) billed by Facebook to company XYZ.
Total_Conversion: Initial inquiries or signups after viewing the ad (Lead stage).
Approved_Conversion: Actual confirmed purchases/paying buyers (Customer stage).
Marketing Metrics Formula Specification:
• Click-Through Rate (CTR): (Clicks / Impressions) × 100%
• Cost Per Click (CPC): Spent / Clicks
• Cost Per Mille (CPM): (Spent / Impressions) × 1,000
• Lead Conversion Rate (Lead CR): (Total_Conversion / Clicks) × 100%
• Sales Conversion Rate (Sales CR): (Approved_Conversion / Clicks) × 100%
• Customer Acquisition Cost (CPA): Spent / Approved_Conversion
• Gross Revenue: Approved_Conversion × Target Customer Value (AOV)
• Campaign Net Profit: Gross Revenue - Spent
• Return on Ad Spend (ROAS): Gross Revenue / Spent
• Campaign ROI (%): ((Gross Revenue - Spent) / Spent) × 100%
Transparency Notice on Dates & Revenue: The original public Facebook dataset records aggregate campaign-level ad exposures rather than continuous calendar day timestamps. Channel is Facebook Ads across 3 strategic campaign phases. Revenue is mathematically modeled from real Approved_Conversion orders multiplied by the customizable Customer Value / AOV slider ($10 to $300, default $100), ensuring transparent, rigorous financial simulation without fabricating ungrounded row-level numbers.
